Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Choose Solo Practice or 2-Player Competition from the start screen. A note appears on the bass-clef staff, and you tap to answer three things: what note it is, which string it falls on, and which finger plays it. The correct answer is shown right after each tap, so kids learn as they go.

Is it really free?

Yes. Cello Flex has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does it collect any personal data?

No. Cello Flex collects no personal data at all. You can read the full Privacy Policy.

What ages is it for?

It's designed for ages 5 and up — beginning cellists and Suzuki students in first position, learning the natural notes.

A note or answer looks wrong, or something isn't working

Please email us at donnyray2000@icloud.com with a quick description (and a screenshot if you can), and we'll look into it right away.
